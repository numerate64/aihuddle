Why we’re here

Less noise.
More useful conversation.

There is no shortage of AI headlines. What’s harder to find is a trusted room where practitioners, leaders, skeptics, and newcomers can compare what they are seeing in the real world.

AI Huddle starts with focused topics and thoughtful facilitation. As the community grows, its questions, experiences, and ideas will shape where we go next.

The weekly huddle

One hour.
Three simple moves.

A relaxed, structured session designed to make room for context, honest discussion, and a useful takeaway.

    ◎

  2. 02

Good to know

Questions,
meet answers.

Who is AI Huddle for?+

Anyone working with, leading, researching, evaluating, or simply trying to better understand AI. You do not need a technical title or years of experience.

Do I need to speak or present?+

No. You are welcome to listen, ask a question in chat, join the discussion, or volunteer to help lead a future topic.

When does the huddle meet?+

The recurring day and time will be announced before launch. Sessions will run weekly for approximately 60 minutes on Zoom.

Is it free?+

Yes. AI Huddle is a free, vendor-neutral community conversation.

Can I suggest or lead a topic?+

Absolutely. Topic suggestions and volunteer facilitators are central to the long-term vision for AI Huddle.
