# AI Huddle Code of Conduct

AI Huddle is a space for useful, respectful conversation among people with different backgrounds, roles, and levels of experience.

## Our commitments

Participants agree to:

- Treat questions and differing viewpoints with respect
- Critique ideas without attacking people
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and demeaning behavior
- Protect private or confidential information shared in the room
- Disclose relevant affiliations and avoid promotional pitches
- Make space for others to speak

## Scope

This code applies to AI Huddle sessions, repository activity, and other community spaces.

## Response

Organizers may redirect, mute, remove, or exclude participants whose behavior makes the space unsafe or consistently undermines the purpose of the community. When a private organizer contact is published, concerns may be reported there. Until then, use a repository issue only for non-sensitive conduct questions; do not post private reports publicly.

This is a concise launch policy and may evolve with community input.
